# Code of Conduct

Sjodur is built by people who want a calm, respectful place to work on software that handles
something personal – other people's money. This document describes how we treat each other
so that everyone can contribute without having to defend their right to be here.

## Our standard

We expect everyone who participates – in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat,
and events connected to the project – to:

- Be kind and direct. Say what you mean, about the work, and assume the other person
  is trying to help.
- Give feedback on code and ideas, never on people. "This query will time out on large
  accounts" is feedback; "you clearly don't understand databases" is not.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new once; questions are not an imposition, and
  "read the docs" is only a helpful answer when it comes with a link.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and time zones. English is
  the project language, but nobody is judged for how well they write it.
- Accept that decisions get made. Disagree with reasons, then commit or step back
  gracefully. Reopening settled discussions without new arguments wears people down.
- Take responsibility. If you got something wrong, say so and fix it. That earns more
  trust than never being wrong.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private: insults, demeaning comments, unwelcome
  sexual attention, stalking, or persistent unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Discrimination or hostility based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
  expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone else's private information – addresses, e-mail, financial details,
  screenshots of private conversations – without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption of discussions and reviews.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

Disagreement is fine. Rudeness is not, and being right does not make it acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces – the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
any chat or mailing list the project runs – and to anyone representing the project in public,
for example when speaking about Sjodur at a meetup or answering questions on its behalf.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this document, or you are unsure
whether something crosses a line, contact the maintainers at **conduct@<your-domain>**.
Reports are handled confidentially; we will not share who reported what beyond the people
who need to know to act on it. Anonymous reports are possible and will still be looked at,
though they may be harder to act on.

You will get an acknowledgement within a few days and be told what happens next.
If a maintainer is the subject of the report, they are excluded from handling it.

## Consequences

Maintainers decide what follows, proportionate to what happened and whether it happened
before:

1. **Correction** – a private note explaining what was inappropriate and why. Sometimes a
   public apology is requested.
2. **Warning** – a formal warning with clear expectations for future conduct, possibly
   combined with a request to avoid interacting with the people involved for a while.
3. **Temporary ban** – no participation in project spaces for a defined period. Breaking
   the ban leads to a permanent one.
4. **Permanent ban** – for a pattern of violations, harassment, or serious hostility towards
   individuals or groups.

Maintainers may also edit, remove, or lock contributions that violate this document, and
will say why.
